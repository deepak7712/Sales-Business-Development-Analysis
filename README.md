# Sales & Business Development Analysis

## Project Overview
This project demonstrates a structured approach to Sales and Business Development by analyzing potential leads, customer segments, deal values, lead sources, and sales pipeline stages.

## Objectives
- Analyze potential customer segments and high-value opportunities.
- Build a structured sales pipeline.
- Track lead generation, qualification, follow-ups, and conversion.
- Compare lead sources and potential deal values.
- Generate insights to improve outreach and engagement.

## Sales Pipeline
1. Lead Generation
2. Initial Contact
3. Qualification
4. Proposal
5. Negotiation
6. Won / Lost
7. Follow-up
